## Why the travel date matters

If you return after the new rules begin, your new I-94 may have a fixed end date instead of D/S. That can add an extension step to your OPT plan. The key choice is whether to travel before filing for OPT or change the travel or filing date. Confirm the sequence with your DSO before you book or file.

## Dates and documents to check

Find the exact program start and end dates on your current I-20. Confirm when your DSO expects to enter the OPT recommendation, the date USCIS should receive your application, and the I-94 issued after any return. If January 10 or February 15 changes, ask your assistant to update the calculation.
