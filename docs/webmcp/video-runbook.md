# HenryKnows WebMCP Demo Video Runbook

Target length: 2:40-2:50. Hard limit: under 3:00.

## Core Idea

Your agent knows you. HenryKnows knows the rules. WebMCP lets them work together.

Use one genuine end-to-end run. Shorten waits in editing, but do not assemble tool outputs from different cases. Place a small `Fictional student scenario` caption on the first demo frame.

## Before Recording

- Use the deployed judging URL in a dedicated ChatGPT Work task.
- Record at 1920x1080 with ChatGPT and the HenryKnows browser panel visible together.
- Hide notifications, personal bookmarks, unrelated tasks, and account details.
- Set readable application zoom before recording and do not change it mid-run.
- Establish the earlier student context in the same task before the recorded segment.
- Complete one rehearsal, reset to a fresh case, and record a second real run.
- Keep the rehearsal only as an emergency backup.

## Earlier Context

Place these facts naturally in one or two messages before the recorded segment:

- Current F-1 student, admitted D/S.
- Current I-20 starts August 25, 2022 and ends December 22, 2026.
- Expects to be in the United States and maintaining F-1 status on September 15, 2026.
- Plans post-completion OPT and hopes to file September 23, 2026.
- Hopes to depart after program completion and return January 10, 2027 on the same SEVIS record while OPT is pending.

The agent may propose these as remembered context, but the student must confirm the consequential facts before calculation.

## Shot List And Narration

### 0:00-0:22 - Stakes And Credibility

Visual: HenryKnows title and ordinary chat interface. Slow zoom toward the product name.

Narration:

> International students are navigating major changes to F-1 rules while managing school, employment, and travel. After more than a decade working in international student services, I built HenryKnows: a primary-source F-1 compliance assistant, with a specialized mapper that shows how the new duration-of-status rules affect an individual student's timeline. WebMCP now lets a student's own AI agent use both tools together.

### 0:22-0:34 - The Missing Context

Visual: Brief view of the earlier planning exchange. Add a small `Fictional student scenario` caption.

Narration:

> This student has already discussed their academic and travel plans with their agent. HenryKnows begins with none of that personal context.

### 0:34-1:12 - The WebMCP Moment

Student prompt:

> Help me figure out whether holiday travel will affect my OPT.

Expected agent move:

> I already know your expected program end date, OPT goal, and holiday travel plan. I can use those details with HenryKnows, show you what I am using, and ask only about anything missing or possibly outdated. Should I proceed?

Student: `Yes.`

Visual priorities:

- Zoom to `ChatGPT and HenryKnows are connected` and the connection pulse.
- Hold while known facts populate the HenryKnows panel.
- Show one genuinely missing or freshness-sensitive question and the student's answer.
- Show the agent's concise recap and one final confirmation.

Narration after confirmation:

> Instead of making the student complete another intake form, the agent brings forward relevant context, asks only what is missing, and keeps the shared case visible for correction.

### 1:12-1:52 - The Collaboration

Visual: Henry consultation, new-rule calculation, reference work, and timeline progress. Accelerate waiting while preserving the order of one real run.

Narration:

> The agent handles the conversation. HenryKnows supplies broad, primary-source regulatory analysis. The specialized mapper calculates how the new rules affect this student's dates. WebMCP keeps all three working from the same confirmed case.

### 1:52-2:18 - Honest Uncertainty

Visual: Zoom to the case-specific unresolved travel and reentry issue.

Narration:

> Here, the available guidance does not squarely resolve this student's planned departure and return while OPT is pending under the new admission rules. HenryKnows does not manufacture certainty. It identifies the gap, explains the practical consequence, and prepares the student to raise the right question with their DSO.

### 2:18-2:42 - Two Useful Outputs

Visual: Documents appear at the top of the panel. Open the student document, download its PDF, then show the concise one-page DSO brief and its primary sources.

Narration:

> The same verified case becomes two different documents: a practical explanation and timeline for the student, and a concise expert briefing for the DSO. The student decides what to share.

### 2:42-2:55 - Close

Visual: Return to the connected split view. End card: `HenryKnows + WebMCP` and the live URL.

Narration:

> A normal website does not know the student. A personal agent does not have HenryKnows' specialized regulatory tools. WebMCP brings them together, so the student does less intake work and arrives better prepared for human advising.

## Editing Restraint

- Use two or three zoom-and-pan cues: connection, live fact collection, final documents.
- Use hard cuts or short dissolves. Avoid decorative transitions.
- Keep captions on, but correct regulatory terms and dates manually.
- Do not add copyrighted music. Clean narration is enough.
- Export at 1080p and verify the final runtime before uploading publicly to YouTube.
