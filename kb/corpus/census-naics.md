North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) — U.S. Census Bureau

NAICS is the standard used by Federal statistical agencies in classifying business establishments for collecting, analyzing, and publishing statistical data on the U.S. business economy. Developed under OMB auspices, adopted 1997 replacing the SIC system; developed jointly by the U.S. ECPC, Statistics Canada, and Mexico's INEGI.

Relevance to DSO practice: NAICS codes are used on Form I-983 (STEM OPT training plans) to classify the student's employer.
