# SEVIS Help Hub

The SEVIS Help Hub is a central location for school officials to access information about SEVIS. Explore the SEVIS Help Hub to find reporting requirements, guides and the latest on SEVIS enhancements and webinars.

## Navigate the SEVIS Help Hub

### [SEVIS Help Hub User Guide](/sevis-help-hub/sevis-help-hub-user-guide)

Navigate all SEVIS Help Hub content. 

### [SEVIS Basics](/sevis-help-hub/sevis-basics)

Learn about important SEVIS pages, SEVIS passwords and data entry standards.

### [School Records](/sevis-help-hub/school-records)

Explore school certification information, including filing initial petitions, updating the Form I-17, “Petition for Approval of School for Attendance by Nonimmigrant Student,” and completing the SEVIS annual verification.

### [Student Records](/sevis-help-hub/student-records)

Find information about creating and maintaining student records, including creating Form I-20, “Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status,” and managing transfers, and student employment.
