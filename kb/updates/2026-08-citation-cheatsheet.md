# Citation Cheat Sheet — the 2026 Rules (Disambiguation)

**Purpose: prevent citation cross-contamination. Two major DHS final rules published three days apart in July 2026, and each has an NPRM. Their citations must never be mixed.**

## Rule 1: Fixed period of admission (the "D/S rule")

- **Final rule: 91 FR 44976** (pages 44976–45131), FR Doc. 2026-14439, DHS Docket No. ICEB-2025-0001, RIN 1653-AA95. Title: "Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission and an Extension of Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors, and Representatives of Foreign Information Media."
- **Published July 17, 2026. Effective September 15, 2026.**
- Its NPRM: **90 FR 42070 (Aug. 28, 2025)** — a proposal only; never cite it as the operative rule.
- Any pinpoint page cite for this rule falls between **44976 and 45131**. A page number of 45324 or higher does NOT belong to this rule.
- Key transition dates derived from this rule: latest date a transition-cohort D/S student can remain without an EOS = program end date or **September 15, 2030** (4 years from the effective date), whichever is first, plus their carried-over 60-day departure period (= **November 14, 2030** outer limit); OPT/STEM OPT I-765-only filing exemption runs through **March 18, 2027** (244 days after publication).

## Rule 2: Public charge rescission

- **Final rule: 91 FR 45324**, FR Doc. 2026-14539, CIS No. 2857-26, RIN 1615-AD06. Title: "Public Charge Ground of Inadmissibility."
- **Published July 20, 2026. Effective September 18, 2026.**
- Rescinds the 2022 Public Charge Rule (**87 FR 55472**), which continues to govern benefits received before September 18, 2026.
- Its NPRM: **90 FR 52168 (Nov. 19, 2025)** — proposal only.
- **91 FR 45324 is a public-charge citation only.** Never attach it to the fixed-period/D/S rule, CPT, OPT, or any SEVIS admission topic.

## Quick reference for other frequently cited items

- **I-901 SEVIS fee:** $350 for F/M students (J-1 $220; reduced $35 for au pair/camp counselor/summer work travel). Set by the 2019 fee rule, **84 FR 23930** (effective June 24, 2019). The 2008 rule (73 FR 55683) is superseded on amounts.
- **CPT scrutiny broadcast:** **SEVP Broadcast Message 2608-01 (Aug. 12, 2026)** — guidance announcing increased CPT scrutiny and DSO liability framing. It is not a rule and does not amend 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(i); cite it as a broadcast message, and attribute its "required for all students / degree impossibility" language to the broadcast, not the regulation.
- **The effective dates are three days apart and easy to swap:** fixed-period rule = **September 15**, 2026; public charge = **September 18**, 2026.
